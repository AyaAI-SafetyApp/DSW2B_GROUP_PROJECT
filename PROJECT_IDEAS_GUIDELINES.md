# Project Ideas Guidelines

## Overview
Each group member should research and present a project idea. We will hold a meeting to vote and select the two best ideas.

## Submission Requirements

### Format Options
You can present your idea in one or both of the following formats:
- **Word Document** (.docx or .pdf)
- **Slide Deck** (.pptx or .pdf)

### Submission Process
1. Place your submission in the appropriate folder:
   - Documents: `project-ideas/documents/`
   - Slides: `project-ideas/slides/`
2. Name your file with the format: `[YourName]_ProjectIdea_[ProjectTitle]`
   - Example: `JohnDoe_ProjectIdea_EcommerceApp.docx`

### Content Requirements
Your project idea should include:
- **Project Title**: Clear and descriptive name
- **Problem Statement**: What problem does this solve?
- **Target Audience**: Who will use this?
- **Key Features**: Main functionality and features
- **Technology Stack**: Proposed technologies/tools
- **Timeline**: Estimated development phases
- **Feasibility**: Why this project is achievable
- **Unique Value**: What makes this project special?

## Voting Process
- All submissions will be reviewed by the group
- Each member can vote for their top 2 choices (excluding their own)
- The two ideas with the most votes will be selected for development
- Voting will be conducted in a group meeting
