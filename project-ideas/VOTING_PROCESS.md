# Project Ideas Voting

## Voting Process

### Before the Meeting
- [ ] All team members submit their project ideas
- [ ] Review all submitted ideas before the meeting
- [ ] Prepare questions for clarification

### During the Meeting
1. **Presentation Phase** (5 minutes per idea)
   - Each member presents their project idea
   - Time for questions and clarification
   
2. **Discussion Phase** (15 minutes)
   - Group discussion about feasibility
   - Consider technical requirements
   - Assess timeline and resources
   
3. **Voting Phase** (10 minutes)
   - Each member votes for their top 2 choices (excluding their own)
   - Anonymous voting recommended
   - Tally votes and announce results

### Voting Criteria
Consider these factors when voting:
- **Technical Feasibility**: Can we realistically build this?
- **Learning Value**: Will this help us grow our skills?
- **Scope**: Is it appropriate for our timeline?
- **Interest Level**: Are we excited about this project?
- **Uniqueness**: Does it offer something new or innovative?

### Voting Template
```
Project Ideas Voting - [Your Name]
Date: [Meeting Date]

Vote 1: [Project Title] by [Author Name]
Reason: [Brief explanation of why you chose this]

Vote 2: [Project Title] by [Author Name]
Reason: [Brief explanation of why you chose this]
```

### After Voting
- [ ] Announce the two winning project ideas
- [ ] Decide on project assignments or team splits
- [ ] Begin planning for the selected projects
- [ ] Archive non-selected ideas for future reference
